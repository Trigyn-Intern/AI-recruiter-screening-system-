# Approval Engine

## Approval required for
- Sending candidate data to external AI providers.
- Changing authentication, authorization, or secret-management behavior.
- Deleting or migrating persisted vector-store data.
- Production deployment, rollback, or release.
- Adding external MCP integrations or third-party services.
- Any action that changes compliance, privacy, cost, or customer-facing behavior.

## Rule
Document the decision, owner, reason, scope, and rollback plan.
