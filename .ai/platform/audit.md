# Audit Requirements

## Record
- Workflow and skills used.
- Files changed and reviewer decisions.
- Test, security, and quality evidence.
- AI-provider selection and whether external data transfer occurred.
- Release artifacts, approvals, and rollback details.
- Exceptions, overrides, and their owners.

## Data protection
Never store candidate resumes, PII, secrets, tokens, or full sensitive prompts in audit records.
