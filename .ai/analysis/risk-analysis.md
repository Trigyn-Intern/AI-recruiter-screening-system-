# Risk Analysis

## Goal
Evaluate product, security, privacy, reliability, and delivery risks.

## Mandatory AI Recruiter risks
- Candidate PII leakage.
- Hosted-model data transfer.
- Prompt injection through resumes or job descriptions.
- Ranking bias, unfair decisions, and insufficient explainability.
- JWT/session compromise.
- Invalid PDF/DOCX uploads.
- Corrupted FAISS index or inconsistent metadata.
- LLM timeout, malformed output, or provider outage.

## Output
For each risk: probability, impact, detection method, prevention, mitigation, owner,
and residual risk.
