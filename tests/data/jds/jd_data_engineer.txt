Data Engineer

Join our data platform team to keep our analytics pipelines fast, trustworthy, and
self-service. You will design batch and streaming architectures and partner with analytics
and product engineering to land trustworthy data in our warehouse.

Responsibilities
- Build batch and streaming pipelines with Airflow, dbt, Spark, and Kafka.
- Model and optimise datasets in PostgreSQL, BigQuery, and Snowflake.
- Maintain our data lake on AWS S3 with Parquet, Iceberg, or Delta layouts.
- Define SLAs, data contracts, and observability with Great Expectations and Monte Carlo.
- Partner with analysts and ML engineers to ship trustworthy features.

Required experience
- 4+ years building production data pipelines.
- Expert SQL and strong Python.
- Hands-on with Spark, Kafka, and Airflow (or Dagster).
- Comfort with one cloud provider (AWS, GCP, or Azure) and Terraform.
- Working knowledge of data modeling (3NF, star schema, slowly changing dimensions).

Nice to have
- Streaming-first systems (Kafka, Flink, Kinesis).
- Experience with dbt, Great Expectations, or DataHub.
- Familiarity with ML feature stores.

Education
- BS in Computer Science, Information Systems, or equivalent experience.
