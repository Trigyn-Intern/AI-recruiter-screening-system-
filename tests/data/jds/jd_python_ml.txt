Senior Python / Machine Learning Engineer

We are looking for a senior engineer to own our Python-based ML services end to end.
You will design training and inference pipelines, ship models to production, and partner
with product teams to turn research ideas into reliable features.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain services in Python 3.11 with FastAPI and pytest coverage.
- Train, evaluate, and deploy classical ML and deep learning models (scikit-learn, PyTorch).
- Design retrieval-augmented features using embeddings, FAISS, and sentence-transformers.
- Operate workloads on AWS (ECS, Lambda, S3, Bedrock) and write clean IaC with Terraform.
- Mentor junior engineers and review pull requests.

Required experience
- 5+ years building production Python systems.
- 3+ years working hands-on with PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Strong SQL and PostgreSQL skills; comfort with OLAP warehouses like BigQuery or Snowflake.
- Hands-on with Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions).
- Solid grounding in linear algebra, statistics, and experimentation.

Nice to have
- LLM fine-tuning, prompt engineering, and evaluation.
- Experience with Airflow or Dagster.
- Familiarity with React for internal tooling.

Education
- BS/MS in Computer Science, Mathematics, or related quantitative field.
